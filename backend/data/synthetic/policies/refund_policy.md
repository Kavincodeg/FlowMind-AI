# Refund Policy

## Purpose
This policy outlines the conditions under which customer refunds are approved, the approval process, and the limits of refund authority at each support level.

## Refund Eligibility Criteria

A refund MAY be approved when ALL of the following are true:
1. The customer has a valid complaint in one of the eligible categories below.
2. The complaint is supported by verifiable evidence (e.g. order records, error logs, delivery reports).
3. The refund request is made within the eligible timeframe.

## Eligible Categories and Timeframes

| Issue Category | Max Refund Eligible | Timeframe from Purchase |
|---|---|---|
| billing | Full charge amount | 60 days |
| delivery | Full order value + shipping | 30 days |
| product_defect | Full product value | 90 days |
| account_access | Pro-rated subscription | Any time (active subscription) |
| service_quality | Partial (goodwill, up to 20%) | 14 days |

## Refund Authority Levels

| Amount | Approval Required |
|---|---|
| Up to $50 | Tier 1 Agent |
| $51 - $200 | Team Lead sign-off |
| $201 - $500 | Team Manager approval |
| $501 - $1000 | Finance & Compliance review |
| Over $1000 | VP Finance approval |

## Refund Process

1. Agent verifies eligibility (category, timeframe, evidence).
2. Agent documents the refund rationale in the ticket.
3. If above agent authority, creates internal refund request ticket and routes to approver.
4. Approver reviews and approves/denies within the SLA for their level.
5. On approval, refund is processed within 5-7 business days.
6. Customer is notified by email with reference number.

## Non-Refundable Situations

- Service usage already consumed.
- Damage caused by customer misuse (documented evidence required).
- Requests outside eligible timeframes without documented exceptional circumstances.
- Tickets where root cause was outside company control (force majeure, third-party outage) — may qualify for credit instead.
